Find a clinic

Find ongoing family doctor/NP attachment separately from walk-in care. Intake statuses refer only to long-term care, not walk-in appointments. No patient-address search.

Care types: This directory was built to find ongoing family physicians and nurse practitioners. It is not an exhaustive directory of walk-in-only clinics. Combined practices are labelled and their walk-in service does not prove roster availability.

How updates work: Every published record includes its last research review date and a public evidence link. The scheduled source monitor checks for changes in public pages and sends discrepancies to a review queue. Only recent, location-specific original clinic notices can receive a green label; automatically published notices require two separate days of consistent evidence. Conflicting findings remain queued for review. Officially listed government program sites may appear with intake unconfirmed; permanent closures require explicit first-party evidence.

Privacy: This page contains public clinic information only. It makes no analytics, tracking, geocoding, or third-party API requests. Links to clinic websites open only when selected. This is not a certified PHIPA-compliant patient-record system, and it must not receive client details.
