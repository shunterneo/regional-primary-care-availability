## Completeness method and results

Cross-checked LDAM, Doctors4Cambridge, BCHS, TVFHT and Thames River FHO lists, Western Schulich’s practice index, regional Healthline records, and targeted CPSO profiles. The Woodstock CPSO Family Doctor search was examined through all seven result pages (69 results). Hospital, hospice, addiction-only and focused dermatology entries were not automatically treated as general primary-care offices. Team offices, physician offices and separate suites were distinguished.

Eighteen offices were added. Seventeen have no established current intake announcement and remain unknown; one publishes applications. New towns are Komoka and Lucan. Their map points are explicitly approximate community points (NRCan Komoka place-name record and Mapcarta Lucan locality), not verified clinic coordinates.

This is **not an exhaustive practitioner census** of every municipality within 75 km. The CPSO Family Doctor filter can omit general practitioners without that specialty, and additional practice locations require separate review. CNO’s Find a Nurse presented a verification gate before search; no CNO completeness claim is made. The teaching index is a source of leads, not a current exhaustive register.
